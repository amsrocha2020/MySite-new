About

From front-end development to AI-assisted quality engineering

António Rocha is a Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) with a strong background in software development and quality engineering. He specializes in building modern test automation solutions using Playwright and TypeScript, helping teams deliver reliable and scalable software with confidence.

Today he is specializing in AI-assisted engineering. He founded and single-handedly built Moneydy, a personal finance SaaS developed with Claude Code and AI agents, with Playwright end-to-end tests generated and maintained with AI — hands-on experience of how AI changes the way software is built and tested.

His career in technology began in 2010 as an OutSystems consultant, where he gained early experience designing and implementing solutions for different clients. This period helped him develop strong problem-solving skills and the ability to adapt quickly to new technological environments.

Between 2014 and 2021, he worked as a Front-End Developer, focusing on building user-friendly and visually engaging web applications. This experience gave him a deep understanding of user experience and interface design, which continues to influence the way he approaches software testing today.

Since 2021, António has focused on quality engineering and test automation, designing testing strategies that improve product reliability and reduce manual regression effort. Currently, he works at Intermedia as a Software Development Engineer in Test, developing automated testing solutions with Playwright and TypeScript, designing test architecture, and integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines.
