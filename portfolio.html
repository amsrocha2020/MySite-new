The product

Expenses, budgets, savings, stocks and crypto rolled up into one live net-worth number. Prices refresh themselves, budgets count down in real time and alerts fire at 80% and 100%. Free and Premium plans in EUR, USD and BRL, with a no-signup live demo.

Built with AI agents

Designed and built end to end by one person with Claude Code. AI agents take on much of the implementation, while António owns the product direction, the architecture and the quality bar.

Tested with AI

Playwright end-to-end tests are generated and maintained with AI, so coverage keeps pace with a codebase that moves at AI speed — an SDET's safety net for AI-written code.
